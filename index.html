Differential Equations and Symmetries

A self contained introduction to the use of symmetries in differential equations. First, we develop the tools necessary by a concise overview of Lie groups and algebras. Then, we use them to study the solvability of differential equations by integration. In the process, we'll find a general algorithm to find symmetries and how to use them to explicitly solve ODEs (when possible). We then draw links to Galois theory and solvability of polynomials. This text ends in an illustration of how to extend these methods to PDEs.
